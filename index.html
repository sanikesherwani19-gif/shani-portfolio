01. Brand Selection

Selecting a brand concept that solves customer problems and provides value.

Brand Concept

Sample brand: EcoLife — an eco-friendly lifestyle brand offering reusable and sustainable products.

Brand Strategy

  • Research the market and competitors.
  • Identify customer needs.
  • Define the brand's unique value.
  • Establish brand goals and positioning.

02. Target Audience Identification

Understanding the people most likely to connect with the brand and its products.

Demographics

  • Age: 18–30 years
  • Audience: Students and young professionals
  • Location: Urban and semi-urban areas
  • Interests: Lifestyle and sustainability

Audience Behaviour

  • Uses Instagram and YouTube.
  • Looks for useful, affordable products.
  • Values honest brand communication.
  • Responds to educational and visual content.
