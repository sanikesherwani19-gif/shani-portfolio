01. Brand Selection

Understanding the brand and its market position.

01

Why Bioderma?

Bioderma is a French dermatological skincare brand known for products designed to support different skin needs and skin types.

02

Brand Positioning

  • Science-led skincare approach
  • Products for different skin concerns
  • Dermatological expertise
  • Focus on skin tolerance and care

02. Target Audience Identification

Identifying the consumers who may be interested in Bioderma products.

Demographics

  • Age: approximately 18–45
  • All genders
  • Skincare beginners and enthusiasts
  • Consumers seeking routine-based skincare

Needs & Behaviours

  • Gentle skincare options
  • Solutions for individual skin concerns
  • Clear product information
  • Reviews and educational skincare content

03. Brand Identity

A visual direction inspired by dermatological care and clarity.

Brand Colour Palette

Blue represents trust and science; white suggests cleanliness; pink and green can provide supporting accents.

Brand Personality

  • Scientific and informed
  • Clean and professional
  • Approachable and caring
  • Focused on skin health

Portfolio tagline: “Science for healthier-looking skin.” This is a creative portfolio line, not an official slogan.

04. Content Planning

Example weekly social media calendar for a skincare brand.

Day Content Type Content Idea
Monday Education How to build a simple skincare routine
Tuesday Stories Skincare questions and polls
Wednesday Reel Demonstration of a cleansing routine
Thursday Product Focus Introduce a product and explain its intended use
Friday Myth vs Fact Share evidence-based skincare information
Saturday Engagement Ask followers about their skincare routines
Sunday Analysis Review reach, saves, shares and comments
